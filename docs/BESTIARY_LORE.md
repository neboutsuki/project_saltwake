# SALTWAKE field guide: creature lore, looks and attacks

How to read this:
- **Look** is written to be easy to generate. Each creature has **one simple silhouette, 3–4 main colors and one
  stand-out feature**. No tiny details are needed. Use the palette from ART_BIBLE.md.
- **Small monsters have 1–2 simple attacks.** Elites add one more.
- **Bosses have basic attacks, 2 special attacks and one ultimate.** You can stop the ultimate by breaking a part.

## Background: what happened to the coast
Three generations ago, the **Red Tide** came in and never fully went out. Its salt stains everything rust-red. What
drinks the brine slowly changes: shells grow over skin, salt crystals grow from bone, and eyes go pale. Ash from the
burning inland forests falls on the coast all year. People who stay in the brine too long become **Brineworn**. They
are still half-aware, and they hate the living because the living remind them of what they lost. Salvagers like you
hunt these creatures for parts, coin and safety, and to find out what the tide is hiding.

# Part 1 · Normal monsters

## Shore and quays

### 1. Gull (existing)
- **Lore:** Ordinary sea birds that learned salvagers mean food. They follow lanterns and open crates.
- **Look:** Grey-white gull with a red-salt stain on its beak; ragged wings.
- **Attacks:** **Peck** (quick dive, small damage) · **Snatch** (steals one item from the ground).

### 2. Salt Crab
- **Lore:** Shore crabs that feed on the red crust. Their shells are half crystal. They sleep buried in the sand and
  wake up all at once.
- **Look:** Small rust-red crab, white salt crystals on its back, one bigger claw.
- **Attacks:** **Pinch** (short melee) · **Pop-up** (bursts out of the sand under you).

### 3. Barnacle Hound
- **Lore:** Former harbor dogs gone feral. Barnacles have grown into their fur like armor. They hunt in packs of
  three, like they once guarded the docks in threes.
- **Look:** Lean grey dog with a back covered in white-grey barnacles; pale eyes.
- **Attacks:** **Bite** (lunge) · **Circle** (two hold back while one attacks from behind).

### 4. Hookjaw Eel
- **Lore:** Eels as long as a rowboat, with a hooked lower jaw. Old fishers say they learned to hunt from the docks
  by watching men drop their nets.
- **Look:** Dark green-black eel, a hooked white lower jaw, a yellow eye.
- **Attacks:** **Lunge** (from the water's edge) · **Drag** (pulls you one step into the water if it grabs you).

### 5. Wrecker (existing)
- **Lore:** Living humans who strip wrecks and dead hunters. Few in number, desperate and cruel. They hate the
  Salvagers' Guild.
- **Look:** Person in rags with a cloth mask, a hook on a rope, a satchel of scrap.
- **Attacks:** **Hook throw** (ranged, pulls you closer) · **Slash** (close knife cut).

### 6. Wrecker Lampman
- **Lore:** Wreckers who once lured ships onto the rocks with false lights. Now they do the same to hunters at
  night.
- **Look:** Wrecker with a tall pole lantern burning a sick green-white; long coat.
- **Attacks:** **False light** (lantern lures you; walking toward it leads to a trap) · **Pole swing** (slow wide
  hit).

### 7. Rope Mimic
- **Lore:** A worm-thing that coils on piers and passes as mooring rope. It tastes of tar and salt.
- **Look:** Coil of brown rope with a hidden small mouth and two tiny pale eyes.
- **Attacks:** **Snare** (grabs your legs and roots you for a moment) · **Squeeze** (damage while rooted).

## Red Salt Flats

### 8. Grazer (existing)
- **Lore:** Herd animals that lick the salt crust. Their panic warns the whole flat that a hunter is near.
- **Look:** Grey-brown deer-like animal with a salt-crusted back and short horns.
- **Attacks:** **Kick** (only when cornered).

### 9. Brineback (existing)
- **Lore:** Big shell-backed grazers. The salt grows their shell bigger every year. Old ones can flatten a cart.
- **Look:** Heavy four-legged beast with a large rust-red domed shell, a small head and stubby legs.
- **Attacks:** **Charge** (straight run, big damage) · **Shell slam** (short-range ground hit).

### 10. Crustback Calf
- **Lore:** Young Brinebacks with soft shells. Their cry carries far, and the adults always answer.
- **Look:** Small round Brineback with a pale, soft pink shell.
- **Attacks:** **Bump** (weak push) · **Cry** (calls a nearby Brineback).

### 11. Salt Skink
- **Lore:** Lizards that bathe in salt to keep cool in the heat. They spit what they have in their mouth: salt.
- **Look:** Slim pale-grey lizard with white speckles, a long tail.
- **Attacks:** **Salt spit** (short-range spray, blinds for a second) · **Dash** (runs away fast).

### 12. Mirage Moth
- **Lore:** Moths as wide as a man's arms. The dust from their wings makes the heat-haze lie. Hunters swear they have
  fought monsters that weren't there.
- **Look:** Big pale moth with eye-spot patterns on its wings and a glittering dust cloud.
- **Attacks:** **Dust cloud** (creates 2–3 fake copies of nearby monsters) · **Flutter** (moves away).

### 13. Crystal Tick
- **Lore:** Ticks that drink salt and swell into hard crystal balls. Crush one and it bursts like glass.
- **Look:** Round red-pink crystal ball with small black legs.
- **Attacks:** **Latch** (sticks on you, drains stamina) · **Burst** (explodes when killed up close).

### 14. Pan Strider
- **Lore:** Wading birds that grew to the height of a horse, walking the evaporation pans. They kick like mules.
- **Look:** Tall grey bird with very long thin legs, a small head, a long beak and a white plume.
- **Attacks:** **Kick** (fast forward kick) · **Beak stab** (long reach).

## Ashwood

### 15. Salt Widow (existing)
- **Lore:** Spiders that spin salt-thread webs between dead pines. The thread hardens like glass in the cold.
- **Look:** Large pale spider with a black body and a red hourglass mark made of salt.
- **Attacks:** **Pounce** (leaps from hiding) · **Web shot** (slows you).

### 16. Widowling
- **Lore:** The young of the Salt Widow. Alone they are nothing. In a swarm they bury you.
- **Look:** Small pale spider, many of them together.
- **Attacks:** **Swarm bite** (small damage, many hits).

### 17. Ash Boar
- **Lore:** Boars that root through the ash for burnt nuts. Their charges kick up clouds you can't see through.
- **Look:** Dark grey boar covered in ash, white tusks, a glowing ember under its skin.
- **Attacks:** **Charge** (straight line, leaves an ash cloud) · **Tusk toss** (throws you back).

### 18. Cinder Owl
- **Lore:** Owls that nest in burnt trees. Their call is so cold it puts out flames. Hunters say it is a bad sign.
- **Look:** Grey owl with ember-orange eyes and wings tipped with ash.
- **Attacks:** **Dive** (silent from above) · **Hush call** (turns your lantern off).

### 19. Pinehusk
- **Lore:** Beetles with bark-like shells that stand still for days and look like stumps.
- **Look:** Brown beetle with a bark-textured shell and two short horns.
- **Attacks:** **Headbutt** (when you walk close) · **Resin spray** (sticky, slows you).

### 20. Ash Stag (Hollow Stag)
- **Lore:** A stag that died in the great fire and didn't stop walking. Its body is hollow and full of ash. It appears
  only at night, and only in fog.
- **Look:** Tall black stag with cracks of pale light in its body and white antlers.
- **Attacks:** **Blink** (vanishes and reappears behind a tree) · **Antler charge** (fast, heavy).

## Reed Marsh

### 21. Reed Lurker
- **Lore:** Long-armed swimmers that live under the reed beds. At night they come up to drag prey under.
- **Look:** Grey-green thin creature with long arms, a wide mouth and two big black eyes.
- **Attacks:** **Grab** (from under the water) · **Drag under** (damage over time until you break free).

### 22. Bog Leech
- **Lore:** Leeches the size of a hand. The salt has made their bite burn.
- **Look:** Fat black leech with a red-ringed mouth.
- **Attacks:** **Latch** (sticks on you and causes bleeding until removed).

### 23. Mire Toad
- **Lore:** Toads full of foul brine. Some hunters milk them for poison.
- **Look:** Fat brown-green toad with a big swollen throat and yellow spots.
- **Attacks:** **Poison spit** (arc shot, leaves a puddle) · **Burst** (explodes in a poison cloud when it dies).

### 24. Silt Heron
- **Lore:** Herons that stand so still they disappear among the reeds. Their beaks go through leather.
- **Look:** Grey heron with a long white spear beak and a dark head crest.
- **Attacks:** **Spear strike** (fast long stab).

### 25. Lantern Fish
- **Lore:** A fish with a light on a stalk. The Lurkers have learned to wait under them.
- **Look:** Small dark fish with a glowing teal ball on a stalk over its head.
- **Attacks:** None. It is bait: a Lurker is often near it.

## Drowned Village · the Brineworn

### 26. Brineworn Drifter (existing)
- **Lore:** The most common Brineworn. Fishers and villagers who stayed too long. They drift toward light and warmth.
- **Look:** Hunched human figure, grey-blue skin, salt crust on the shoulders, pale empty eyes, rotten fisher clothes.
- **Attacks:** **Claw** (slow swipe) · **Grab** (holds you for a moment).

### 27. Brineworn Caller (existing)
- **Lore:** Brineworn who still remember their voices. Their song calls the others.
- **Look:** Thin Brineworn with an open mouth and a small rusty bell around its neck.
- **Attacks:** **Call** (summons 1–2 Drifters) · **Scream** (short knockback).

### 28. Brineworn Breaker (existing)
- **Lore:** Big men, dock workers and smiths, whose bodies grew huge in the brine. They break doors and walls.
- **Look:** Huge hunched Brineworn with one giant salt-crusted arm.
- **Attacks:** **Slam** (slow, big ground hit) · **Smash** (breaks cover).

### 29. Brineworn Crawler
- **Lore:** The ones who drowned and came back without legs. They move fast along floors and under water.
- **Look:** Legless Brineworn pulling itself on long arms, a salt-crusted back.
- **Attacks:** **Leap** (from the floor or water) · **Bite**.

### 30. Brineworn Bloater
- **Lore:** Brineworn who swallowed too much brine. They swell until they burst.
- **Look:** Round swollen Brineworn, pale-green skin, tiny head.
- **Attacks:** **Walk-up burst** (walks to you and explodes into a poison pool).

### 31. Brineworn Tidewife
- **Lore:** Women of the drowned village who still wait for the boats. Their wail sounds like the sea.
- **Look:** Tall thin Brineworn in a long wet veil and a torn dress, dripping water.
- **Attacks:** **Wail** (cone; soaks you and slows you) · **Claw**.

### 32. Bell Hulk
- **Lore:** A Breaker who hid in the bell tower when the tide came. The bell grew into his body.
- **Look:** Huge Brineworn with a big green bronze bell fused onto his back and shoulders.
- **Attacks:** **Toll** (ring shockwave, stuns) · **Slam**.

## Caves, cliffs and storm coast

### 33. Salt Bat
- **Lore:** Cave bats that roost in salt grottos. Light hurts them.
- **Look:** Small grey bat with pale-pink wings.
- **Attacks:** **Swoop** (small hit, in swarms).

### 34. Gale Kite
- **Lore:** Cliff hawks that hunt only in storms, riding the wind like it's a ladder.
- **Look:** Dark grey hawk with long pointed wings and a white stripe.
- **Attacks:** **Dive** (fast straight strike from the air).

### 35. Stonejaw
- **Lore:** Crab-turtles that wedge themselves into the cliff and wait. Some have been there longer than the town.
- **Look:** Grey rock-like shell with a crab face and two big flat claws.
- **Attacks:** **Clamp** (slow, very strong pinch) · **Rock spit** (throws a stone).

### 36. Brine Slime
- **Lore:** A puddle of brine that moves. Nobody knows if it is alive.
- **Look:** Blob of murky green-teal liquid with small floating salt crystals.
- **Attacks:** **Splash** (touch damage) · **Split** (becomes two small slimes when hit).

### 37. Blackwater Thing
- **Lore:** Seen only in lightning flashes, standing in black water. It is never there when you look again.
- **Look:** Tall thin black silhouette with long arms and no face.
- **Attacks:** **Flash step** (moves only during lightning) · **Reach** (long arm strike).

## Elite variants (simple color change + one extra move)
| Variant | Look | Extra move |
|---|---|---|
| **Scarred** | old scars, one missing eye | a stronger version of its main attack |
| **Red-crusted** | thick red salt armor | armor must break before it takes full damage |
| **Ashborn** | dark grey, ash smoking off it | ash cloud around it |
| **Tide-touched** | wet, glowing teal | stronger in water, heals in rain |
| **Starving** | thin, ribs showing, red eyes | faster, never flees |

# Part 2 · Bosses

Each boss has **basic attacks** (simple, frequent), **2 special attacks** (big, telegraphed) and **one ultimate**
(one-hit kill; stop it by breaking the listed part).

### B1. Tidemaw — Outer Quays · Boathouse Slip
- **Lore:** The thing under the Boathouse Slip. The fishers fed it their catch for years to keep it quiet. When the
  boats stopped coming, it came looking.
- **Look:** Huge eel body with a crab-armored head, glowing teal gill sacs on both sides of the neck, barnacled grey
  hide.
- **Basic:** **Bite** (lunge) · **Tail sweep** (behind it).
- **Special:** **Burrow strike** (dives into sand, bursts out under you) · **Brine spit** (3-shot spread; missed
  shots leave harpoon wrecks to throw back).
- **Ultimate:** **Riptide**: floods the whole slip. **Break both gills** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** gills ×2, jaw plate, tail.

### B2. Brineback Matriarch — Red Salt Flats · Evaporation Pans
- **Lore:** The oldest Brineback, her shell covered in a crown of salt. Her calves ride on her back.
- **Look:** Giant Brineback with a jagged salt crown on her shell and small calves clinging to her sides.
- **Basic:** **Charge** · **Stomp**.
- **Special:** **Triple charge** (three charges in a row; bait her into salt pillars to stun her) · **Calf call**
  (sends calves at you).
- **Ultimate:** **Shell Quake**: slam that hits the whole arena. **Break the crown** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** crown, foreleg plates.

### B3. Salt Widow Queen — Ashwood · Burnt Clearing
- **Lore:** Mother of every widow in the Ashwood. She hides in the burnt clearing her own fire made.
- **Look:** Giant pale spider, black body, glowing red hourglass, a big swollen egg sac.
- **Basic:** **Fang strike** · **Web shot**.
- **Special:** **Brood burst** (releases Widowlings) · **Web lunge** (jumps on a web line; missed webs become ropes).
- **Ultimate:** **Silk Tomb**: covers the arena in webs and pulls you in. **Burn the spinnerets** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** fangs, spinnerets, egg sac.

### B4. The Caller Choir — Drowned Village · Flooded Chapel
- **Lore:** Five Callers who sang together for so long they fused into one body around an old lantern.
- **Look:** Mound of five grey Brineworn bodies joined together, their mouths open, holding up a green lantern.
- **Basic:** **Scream wave** · **Arm swipe**.
- **Special:** **Summon** (Drifters climb out of the water) · **Choir beam** (sound beam that sweeps).
- **Ultimate:** **Requiem**: endless summons until you die. **Break the lantern-lure** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** lantern-lure, singer throats ×5.

### B5. Gale Harrier — Storm Cliffs · Lamp Gallery
- **Lore:** A storm bird as big as a boat that nests in the lighthouse. Sailors believe it brings the squalls.
- **Look:** Huge dark grey raptor, long wings with white stripes, a sharp yellow beak.
- **Basic:** **Talon swipe** · **Wing gust** (pushes you back).
- **Special:** **Storm spit** (lightning ball; reflect it with the lighthouse mirror) · **Feather rain** (rain of
  sharp feathers).
- **Ultimate:** **Thunderdive**: one-hit dive. **Break a wing** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** wings ×2, talons.

### B6. Salt Wyrm — Brine Gate · Tide Throne (story final)
- **Lore:** The heart of the Red Tide. A serpent of living salt that has slept under the sea for a hundred years.
- **Look:** Huge serpent made of red-pink salt crystal, sharp crystal spines down its back, glowing white eyes.
- **Basic:** **Bite** · **Coil crush**.
- **Special:** **Crystal shards** (shards that shatter and can be thrown back) · **Salt breath** (cone that turns you
  to stone if you stay in it).
- **Ultimate:** **Crystal Tide**: the floor becomes spikes. **Break the spines** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** spines ×3, eyes, heart-geode.

### B7. Reed Mother — Reed Isle · Lurker Pool
- **Lore:** The first Reed Lurker. All the others are her children.
- **Look:** Huge pale-green Lurker with a long lure stalk over her head, mostly under water.
- **Basic:** **Grab** · **Tail splash**.
- **Special:** **Lure flash** (blinds you) · **Child call** (Reed Lurkers rise from the pool).
- **Ultimate:** **Drowning**: drags you to the bottom. **Cut the lure stalk** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** lure stalk, jaw.

### B8. Hollow Elk-King — Lost Grove · Pine Hollow (night only)
- **Lore:** King of the hollow stags. His antlers never stopped burning after the great fire.
- **Look:** Giant black elk, cracks of pale light in its body, burning orange antler crown.
- **Basic:** **Antler charge** · **Stomp**.
- **Special:** **Blink** (teleports between trees; burn the trees) · **Ember rain** (burning embers fall).
- **Ultimate:** **Ash Eclipse**: total darkness and a charge. **Break the crown** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** antler crown, front legs.

### B9. The Drowned Captain — Wreck Isle · Hull Graveyard
- **Lore:** Captain of the last ship to leave the harbor. He came back alone, fused with his ship's figurehead.
- **Look:** Huge Brineworn with a wooden figurehead grown into his chest, an anchor for one arm, a lantern on his
  belt.
- **Basic:** **Anchor swing** · **Kick**.
- **Special:** **Anchor throw** (missed throws pin him for a hit window) · **Crew call** (Brineworn sailors).
- **Ultimate:** **Broadside**: cannon salvo across the arena. **Break the anchor arm** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** figurehead, anchor arm, lantern.

### B10. Mill Leviathan — Reed Isle · Sunken Mill
- **Lore:** A toad that grew in the mill pond until the mill sank into its back.
- **Look:** Giant brown-green toad with a broken waterwheel stuck in its back.
- **Basic:** **Tongue whip** · **Belly bump**.
- **Special:** **Poison spit** (big puddles) · **Swallow** (eats a Bloater, then spits it at you).
- **Ultimate:** **Deluge**: belly flop that floods the mill. **Jam the wheel** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** waterwheel, vocal sac, tongue.

### B11. Ashen Wyvern — Ashwood isle · Cinder Peak
- **Lore:** A wyvern that lives on the edge of the inland fires. It carries embers in its throat.
- **Look:** Grey winged lizard, glowing orange throat, tail with a spike.
- **Basic:** **Bite** · **Tail spike**.
- **Special:** **Ember breath** (fire line) · **Dive bomb** (lands with an ash cloud).
- **Ultimate:** **Firestorm**: burns the whole arena. **Pop the throat sac** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** wings ×2, throat sac, tail spike.

### B12. Bone Tide Hermit — Shore isle · Shell Midden
- **Lore:** A hermit crab that found the skull of a whale and never left it.
- **Look:** Big red crab living in a huge white whale skull, one giant claw, eyes on stalks.
- **Basic:** **Claw slam** (leaves rocks you can throw) · **Pinch**.
- **Special:** **Hide** (retreats into the skull, armored) · **Bubble spit**.
- **Ultimate:** **Skull Roll**: rolls around the arena. **Break the skull** to stop it.
- **Breakable:** skull shell, big claw, eye stalks.

## Roaming bosses (appear at random)

### R1. Wrecker Warlord "Rook"
- **Lore:** The last captain of the Wreckers. Clever, cruel and very human.
- **Look:** Tall Wrecker in a long black coat, a crow-feather mask, a heavy crossbow.
- **Basic:** **Bolt shot** · **Knife combo**.
- **Special:** **Trap net** (throws a net trap) · **Gang call** (Wreckers).
- **Ultimate:** **Last stand**: explosive barrels around the arena. **Break his powder belt** to stop it.

### R2. Storm Rider
- **Lore:** A smaller, wilder Gale Harrier that follows storms from map to map.
- **Look:** Gale Harrier with a black-blue body and lightning-blue stripes.
- **Basic:** **Dive** · **Gust**.
- **Special:** **Lightning call** (strikes where it marks the ground).
- **Ultimate:** **Eye of the storm**: lightning everywhere. **Break its crest** to stop it.

### R3. The Lantern Thief
- **Lore:** Nobody has seen it. Lanterns go out where it walks, and they find hunters in the dark later.
- **Look:** Tall thin grey figure in a cloak of stolen, dead lanterns.
- **Basic:** **Claw** · **Douse** (turns your lantern off).
- **Special:** **Shadow step** (only moves in the dark).
- **Ultimate:** **Blackout**: total darkness and a strike. **Break its lanterns** to stop it.

### R4. Red Tide Swarm
- **Lore:** During a red tide, the Salt Crabs pile on each other until they become one giant body.
- **Look:** A huge crab shape made of hundreds of small red Salt Crabs.
- **Basic:** **Claw** · **Wave** (crabs flood forward).
- **Special:** **Split** (breaks into small crabs, then joins again).
- **Ultimate:** **Red Flood**: crab wave across the arena. **Break the queen crab** in its core to stop it.

## Apex (rare, after the story)

### A1. Salt Wyrm Elder
- **Lore:** The Salt Wyrm's mother, older than the coast.
- **Look:** Salt Wyrm but white-gold crystal, twice the size.
- **Attacks:** the Salt Wyrm's moves, faster, plus **Crystal storm** (shards from above).

### A2. The Deepbell
- **Lore:** Something huge under the sea that rings the drowned bells. Every Brineworn turns toward it.
- **Look:** Colossal bell-shaped jellyfish-thing of green bronze with long dark tendrils.
- **Attacks:** **Toll wave** (stun) · **Tendril sweep** · **Ultimate: Last Bell**. **Break the clapper** to stop it.

### A3. First Brineworn
- **Lore:** The first person changed by the tide. The answer to what the Red Tide really is.
- **Look:** Human-sized, pale and calm, crowned with salt crystals, standing in a pool of red water.
- **Attacks:** uses one move from every Brineworn type, then **Ultimate: Return to the sea**. **Break its crown** to
  stop it.
