# SALTWAKE B18 – Ren Kagari walking draft

Four original image-generated six-frame walk cycles, 24 individual 64×64 RGBA frames, four horizontal 384×64 PNG strips, four GIF previews and the original atlas. The art follows the approved red-scarf story character reference, not the earlier generic hooded salvager. No legacy assets are overwritten.

Frame origin (32, 60) and 120 ms per frame are provisional defaults. All directions use separately generated artwork; no mirroring or repeated copies. Front/back stepping may be subtle and the scarf may vary frame-to-frame. Visual animation, anchor stability, collision, camera angle, and in-game timing must still be reviewed. Idle, combat, hit/death, run, dodge, and other characters are not included. Two generated presentation boards were excluded.
