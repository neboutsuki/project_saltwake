# SALTWAKE bestiary (draft v1)

Lore rules this list follows:
- The world is drained by ash and poisoned by **brine**: the red salt and the tide mutate what they touch.
- **Brineworn** are mutated former humans and attack humans. **Living hostile humans stay rare** (Wreckers and a few
  cults only).
- Most creatures are **animals reshaped by salt, ash or tide**, not fantasy races. Night belongs to the worst of
  them.
- Threat 1 is a nuisance, 3 is a real hunt, and 5 is an apex.

Status: **existing** = already in the game; everything else is new.

### Elite variants (on any map, rarer, better loot)
- **Scarred:** a veteran with more health and one extra move.
- **Red-crusted:** salt armor that must be broken first, and resistant to blades.
- **Ashborn:** an ash cloud around it lowers visibility.
- **Tide-touched:** stronger in water, heals in rain.
- **Starving:** fast and reckless, attacks even when outnumbered.

Each boss has at least 2 gimmicks, breakable parts, 2–3 phases and a one-hit ultimate that you can prevent by breaking
the right part (see GAME_LOOP_DESIGN.md §7–9).

3. Salt Widow Queen + Widowling, Ash Boar, Cinder Owl.
